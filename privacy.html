Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Who uses Perpetuum

Perpetuum is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to other users.

What data we access

  • Basic profile information of the owner's account, such as the account ID and username, provided by the platform during sign-in.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram and YouTube after the owner signs in.
  • Videos and captions created by the owner, which are uploaded to the owner's own accounts.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties. Videos intended for publication may be hosted publicly so that the platform can retrieve them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about viewers or other users.
  • We do not sell or share any data.
  • We do not use data for advertising profiles.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Perpetuum's access at any time in the security or connected apps settings of TikTok, Instagram or Google.
