Perpetuum

Perpetuum is a personal content tool. It generates short vertical videos with text and helps the owner publish them to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

How it works

  1. The owner generates a short video on their own computer.
  2. The owner signs in to their own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account through the official login of each platform.
  3. The tool uploads the video through the platform's official API. On TikTok, the video is sent to the owner's inbox as a draft, and the owner reviews, edits and posts it inside the TikTok app.

Perpetuum is not offered to the public and does not access accounts of other people.
